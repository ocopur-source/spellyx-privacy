Welcome to Spellyx ("we," "us," or "our"). We respect your privacy and are committed to being transparent about how we handle information when you use our application. This Privacy Policy explains what data is collected, why it is collected, and how you can exercise your rights.

Short version: Spellyx is a word-learning game. We do not sell your personal data. We use third-party services (Google AdMob, Firebase) to display ads, analyse crashes, and improve the app experience.
Section 01

Information We Collect

We collect two categories of information: information you provide directly and information collected automatically.

Automatically collected information may include:

  • Device identifiers (advertising ID, device model, OS version)
  • App usage data (screens visited, features used, session duration)
  • Crash and performance diagnostics (error logs, stack traces)
  • Approximate location (country/region level, derived from IP address)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks — collected by ad partners)

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any account credentials, as Spellyx does not require user registration.

Section 02

How We Use Your Data

The information we collect is used solely to operate, maintain, and improve Spellyx:

  • Serving and personalising advertisements through Google AdMob
  • Monitoring app stability and fixing crashes via Firebase Crashlytics
  • Understanding how users interact with features (Firebase Analytics)
  • Delivering remote configuration updates (Firebase Remote Config)
  • Sending optional local notifications (push reminders you can disable)

We do not use your data to build individual user profiles for sale, or to make automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects on you.

Section 04

Advertising & Consent (GDPR / EEA)

Spellyx displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob. These ads may be personalised based on your interests if you grant consent, or non-personalised if you do not.

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), UK, or Switzerland, we use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request your consent before serving personalised ads. You can withdraw or change your consent at any time through the app's Settings → Privacy & Ads menu.

No consent = non-personalised ads. If you decline or are outside the EEA, you will still see ads, but they will not be tailored to your interests.

Ads may use cookies and similar technologies on supported platforms. For more information on how Google uses advertising data, visit policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Section 05

Data Retention & Storage

Game progress, settings, and preferences are stored locally on your device only. We do not maintain a server-side database of personal user records.

Analytics and crash data collected by Firebase are retained in accordance with Google's default retention policies (typically 14 months for analytics events). You may request deletion as described in Section 6.

Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data from your device.

Section 06

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of access — request a copy of data we hold about you
  • Right to rectification — request correction of inaccurate data
  • Right to erasure — request deletion of your personal data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Right to restriction — request that we limit processing of your data
  • Right to data portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Right to object — object to processing based on legitimate interests or for direct marketing
  • Right to withdraw consent — withdraw consent for personalised ads at any time in Settings

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the address in Section 9. We will respond within 30 days.

California residents (CCPA): You have the right to know, delete, and opt-out of the sale of your personal information. We do not sell personal information. To submit a request, email us.
Section 07

Children's Privacy

Spellyx is intended for general audiences and is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal data to us, please contact us immediately at ocopur@yahoo.com and we will take steps to delete such information.

Section 08

Continued use of Spellyx after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
